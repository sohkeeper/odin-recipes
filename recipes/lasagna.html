Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 450g ground beef
  • marinara sauce
  • lasagna noodles
  • oz ricotta cheese
  • shredded mozzarella cheese
  • grated parmesan cheese
  • egg

Instructions

  1. Prep the meat: Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat. Drain the fat, then stir in the marinara sauce.
  2. Mix the cheese: In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the parmesan cheese.
  3. Layer the lasagna In an 8x12-inch baking dish, layer the components in this order:
  4. Bottom:A thin layer of meat sauce
    1. Layer 1:3 noodles, 1/3 of the ricotta mixture, and 1/3 of the mozzarella
    2. Layer 2:Sauce, 3 noodles, 1/3 ricotta, and 1/3 mozzarella.
    3. layer 3: Sauce, 3 noodles, remaining ricotta, remaining mozzarella, and remaining parmesan.
  5. Bake:Cover the dish with foil. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  6. RestLet it cool for 10 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
